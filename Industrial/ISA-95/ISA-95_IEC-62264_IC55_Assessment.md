# ISA-95/IEC 62264 Enterprise-Control System Integration Fundamentals (IC55)

12 questions for assessing the understanding of ISA-95

*Source: [IC55 Knowledge Check](https://forms.gle/nNHGCWJnQkWBJQkh7)*

1. The ISA-95 standards is needed because <u>Integrating logistics systems to manufacturing is hard to do</u>.
2. What are three functions the SCOR supply chain model defines that are present in each link of the chain? <u>Source, make and deliver</u>
3. What are thet two principal domains called out in ISA-95? <u>Enterprise and control</u>
4. What is the hierarchical level that is generally presented at the "top" in ISA-95's context? <u>Business planning and logistics</u>
5. Which of the following is NOT one of the information categories identified in ISA-95? <u>Sales incentive information</u>
6. The operations performance category of information shows what <u>was made and what was actually used to make it.</u>
7. The four categories of manufacturing operations named in the Level 3 MOM models are <u>Production, maintenance, quality and inventory</u>.
8. In the generic 8-bubble activity model, the group of activities that manage information about the resources required by operations, and the relationships between those resources, is called the <u>Resource management</u>.
9. Which generic level 3 activity model sends operational commands and receives responses from direct control systems at level 1 and 2? <u>Execution management</u>
10. In the generic activity model, which activity takes the production schedule from level 4 and determines the optimal use of local resources to meet production schedule requirements? <u>Detailed production scheduling</u>
11. What are the two main element areas in an ISA-95, part 5 message? <u>Application identification and data areas</u>
12. The verbs, that may act upon information object nouns in an ISA-95 action request, include <u>get, show, change and confirm</u>.
