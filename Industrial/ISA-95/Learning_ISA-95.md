## 1. Why ISA-95 (as an ontology)?

ISA-95 provides a universal language for all manufacturing activities that fall within its scope. This language can facilitate human and digital communication.

- As a vocabulary, it provides a precise terminology to discuss requirements and implementations.
- As a data model, it provides a standard schema for your computer systems to store and exchange data.

Adoption of ISA-95:
- removes ambiguity,
- decreases implementation time, and,
- utimately, creates a data foundation for a smarter, more connected manufacturing operations.

### The Scope of ISA-95

ISA-95 covers everything that happens in a manufacturing operation between **business planning** and **pure process controls**.

ISA-95 part 3 defines eight activities that comprise manufacturing operations. These 8 activities fall into four phases:

- **Reference**: The resource and work definition models provide the foundational reference data for systems to schedule, execute, and analyze manufacturing production.
- **Planning**: Detailed scheduling plans productions at a high-level; dispatching plans production at a faster pace and in more granular detail.
- **Execution**: These models record informationa bout production as it executes.
- **Analysis**: Finally, after production is recorded, your systems can use the recorded data to track goods and measure performance.
