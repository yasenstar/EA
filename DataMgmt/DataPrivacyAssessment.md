Personal Data refers to any iformation about a living natural person who can be identified, either directly through details like a name or ID number, or indirectly by combining pieces of information that together point to a specific individual.

Personal Data is information that relates to and can identify a living person and therefore reuqires careful handling.

Personal Data in our work:
- Employee data
- Vehicle data
- Business partner data

Main principles of Personal Data:
- Need and purpose
- Secure handling
- Delete when done

Sample: just three basic data points -- gender, ZIP code, and date of birth -- can uniquely identify a large portion of individuals in the United States.

Self assessment questions:
1. Could the data I'm collecting or using be linked to a person, making it Personal Data?
2. Do I really need all this Personal Data?
3. Am I allowed to use this Personal Data?
4. What minimum information is needed for this task?
5. How do I safeguard the Personal Data I need to use?

We act as either **Data Controller** or **Data Processor** role:
- Data Controller: defines why and how Personal Data is used and ensures it is handled lawfully and compliantly. Accountable for the processing, including any Data Processors.
- Data Processor: processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller, following instructions, protecting data, and demonstrating compliance as required.

Personal Data handling process:
1. Define Privacy Scope
2. Assess Privacy Impact
3. Design to Meet Requirements
4. Update Volvo Group RoPA
